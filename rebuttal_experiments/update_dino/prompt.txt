## Scientific context

RAFM decomposes a vector into radius and direction. It samples the source radius from the empirical training radial law and transports directions using norm-preserving spherical geodesics.

The central claim to test on images is not merely “spherical paths help.” It is:

> source design and transport geometry should be chosen jointly.

The required source/path decomposition is:

1. Gaussian source + Euclidean path: standard Euclidean Flow Matching.
2. Empirical matched-radial source + Euclidean path: source-only ablation.
3. Fixed-radius spherical source + spherical path: SFM/RFM/RJF-style angular-only baseline.
4. Empirical matched-radial source + spherical path: RAFM.

The reviewer specifically suggested testing RAFM in DINO/representation-autoencoder latents, where recent work normalizes representations onto spheres and learns angular flow. The experiment must determine whether preserving a non-degenerate empirical radial law improves over a uniform/fixed-radius spherical prior, or whether DINO/RAE latents are effectively fixed-radius and RAFM reduces to the spherical baseline.

Do not assume there is useful radial variation. Measure it first.

4. Download a released RAE or RJF Stage-2 checkpoint and verify sampling/evaluation end-to-end.

Required tests:

- SLERP endpoints are exact.
- SLERP preserves radius, including a stable small-angle branch.
- Tangent projection is orthogonal to the state.
- The manifold integrator preserves radius within numerical tolerance.
- Empirical radial CDF uses training data only.
- No validation/test radius enters source construction.
- Fixed-radius baseline uses the training-set mean radius only.
- All source/path variants use identical neural architecture and parameter count.
- Same seed/config reproduces identical batches and initialization.
- Metrics distinguish standard generation metrics from post-hoc common-radial diagnostics.

Do not begin expensive training until these tests pass.

# Phase 2 — Representation geometry diagnostics (mandatory before training)

Measure the exact latent tensor consumed by the Stage-2 generative model. Do not analyze a convenient tensor that is different from the actual Stage-2 input.

Primary representation:

- official RAE DINOv2-B encoder + pretrained decoder.

Inspect, when accessible:

1. raw encoder output before RAE normalization;
2. normalized tensor passed to Stage 2;
3. token-level vectors;
4. the flattened global latent vector.

For train and validation splits, compute:

- global norm distribution;
- per-token norm distribution;
- mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation, skewness, kurtosis;
- train/validation radial `W1` and KS;
- class-conditional norm distributions;
- between-class variation of mean radius;
- dependence between radius and direction/class;
- radial correlations across spatial tokens;
- effective dimensionality and covariance anisotropy.

Perform decoder sensitivity diagnostics:

- multiply latent radii by factors such as
  `0.50, 0.75, 0.90, 1.00, 1.10, 1.25, 1.50`;
- preserve direction;
- decode;
- measure rFID, LPIPS, PSNR and SSIM where supported;
- save uncurated reconstructions using fixed images and scales.

Produce:

1. global and token norm histograms;
2. norm coefficient-of-variation table;
3. class-conditional radius plot;
4. decoder quality versus radius scale;
5. representative reconstructions at several scales.

Decision rule:

- If the Stage-2 DINO/RAE latent has negligible radial variability, explicitly label it a **fixed-radius / angular-only control**. Do not manufacture a radial advantage.
- If raw/pre-normalization representations vary radially but cannot be passed through the released decoder, do not present them as a generative benchmark.
- If a non-degenerate decodable radial law exists, continue with the full RAFM comparison.

Also determine whether the baseline geometry is a single global sphere or a product of token-wise spheres. The primary RAFM implementation must match the baseline geometry. A token-wise radius-field extension may be explored, but it must be clearly labeled as an extension rather than silently replacing original global RAFM.

# Phase 3 — CIFAR-10 controlled benchmark

Run a relatively inexpensive controlled image benchmark before full ImageNet training.

Dataset:

- CIFAR-10, official 50,000-image training split.
- No test leakage.
- Three seeds for the main comparison.
- Generate 50,000 samples per final model for reported metrics.

Use one common architecture, optimizer, learning-rate schedule, EMA, augmentations, batch size, training steps, initialization protocol, and evaluation pipeline across all methods.

Required methods:

1. `gaussian_euclidean`
   - Gaussian source;
   - linear/Euclidean conditional path.

2. `matched_euclidean`
   - empirical train-only radial source;
   - uniform random direction;
   - Euclidean path;
   - source-only ablation.

3. `fixed_spherical`
   - fixed radius equal to training mean radius;
   - uniform direction;
   - spherical geodesic path;
   - fixed-radius SFM/RFM baseline.

4. `rafm_empirical`
   - empirical train-only radial source;
   - uniform direction;
   - spherical geodesic path;
   - tangent-preserving inference.

Optional only after the core four are complete:

5. smoothed log-radius KDE source;
6. OT-CFM / spherical OT baselines;
7. a learned norm-refinement network.

Do not tune each method independently on the test set. Use a shared validation protocol and document every method-specific choice.

Metrics:

- gFID/FID-50k, lower is better;
- sFID if supported;
- Inception Score;
- precision and recall;
- radial `W1` and KS;
- global sliced `W1`;
- normalized-direction sliced `W1`;
- common-radial sliced `W1` as a post-hoc direction-only diagnostic;
- train time, sampling time, samples/second;
- NFE;
- peak VRAM;
- parameter count;
- analytical leading-order FLOP estimate.

Sampling-efficiency sweeps:

- at minimum NFE `10, 20, 50, 100`;
- use appropriate Euclidean and geometry-preserving integrators;
- report both NFE and measured wall-clock;
- do not call a cross-method quality gap “solver efficiency.”

Save fixed-seed, uncurated sample grids for all methods using the same latent seeds and class labels where applicable.

# Phase 4 — Core ImageNet-256 RAE/DINO experiment

This is the main modern deep-learning experiment.

Use:

- ImageNet-1K at 256×256;
- frozen official DINOv2-B RAE encoder/decoder;
- the exact normalized Stage-2 latent representation;
- LightningDiT-B as the first full comparison;
- class-conditional generation;
- official ADM FID reference statistics;
- 50,000 generated images for final metrics.

Base the implementation on official RAE and RJF code rather than rewriting the whole training stack.

## Main four-way factorial comparison

Keep architecture and training budget fixed:

1. Euclidean FM:
   - Gaussian source;
   - linear path.

2. Source-only:
   - empirical train-only radial source;
   - Euclidean path.

3. Fixed-radius spherical:
   - uniform/fixed-radius source;
   - spherical geodesic path;
   - no radial modeling.

4. RAFM:
   - empirical train-only radial source;
   - spherical geodesic path.

For class-conditional ImageNet:

- primary source law: global train radial law;
- optional ablation: class-conditional radial law, only if each class has adequate data;
- clearly separate global and class-conditional results;
- never use validation radii to define either law.

If the upstream RJF objective includes Jacobi regularization, do not confound it with radial source choice. Run either:

- the source comparison with Jacobi weighting disabled for both spherical variants; and then
- a paired ablation with the same Jacobi weighting enabled for both fixed-radius spherical and RAFM;

or provide an equally rigorous design that isolates the radial source effect.

## Training protocol

First run:

1. a one-batch overfit test;
2. a short smoke run;
3. a pilot with frequent checkpoint evaluation.

Only then launch the full run.

Use the upstream protocol unless hardware requires a documented adaptation. A reasonable target based on the official RAE/RJF setup is:

- LightningDiT-B;
- 80 epochs;
- global batch approximately 1024 when feasible through DDP/gradient accumulation;
- AdamW, learning rate `2e-4`, betas `(0.9, 0.95)`;
- EMA;
- gradient clipping;
- FP32 when required for stability;
- 50-step primary sampling;
- equal class-label sampling: 50 samples per class for FID-50k.

Do not claim an 80-epoch full ImageNet result if training used a subset or fewer epochs.

Compute-adaptive rule:

- Full ImageNet may be reported with one full seed because of cost.
- Evaluate multiple fixed checkpoints to show convergence.
- Use three seeds for CIFAR and for short ImageNet pilots when feasible.
- If ImageNet access or compute is unavailable, record the blocker immediately, complete CIFAR and latent diagnostics, and optionally run a clearly labeled ImageNet-100/subset pilot. Never label a subset as ImageNet-1K.

MSGM is optional on ImageNet only if computationally tractable. Do not spend weeks reproducing a baseline whose complexity makes the comparison infeasible. Measure and report the feasibility estimate honestly.

## ImageNet metrics

Primary:

- gFID/FID-50k;
- sFID;
- Inception Score;
- precision;
- recall.

Secondary:

- latent radial `W1`, KS, global sliced `W1`;
- normalized-direction and common-radial sliced `W1`;
- per-class FID or class-conditional diagnostics where feasible;
- top-1/top-5 accuracy of a fixed pretrained classifier on generated images as an auxiliary semantic metric;
- NFE, wall-clock, throughput, VRAM, FLOPs.

Also report rFID/LPIPS/PSNR for autoencoder reconstruction and radius-scaling diagnostics. Do not mix reconstruction FID (`rFID`) with generation FID (`gFID`).

# Phase 5 — Non-degenerate image-latent control

The DINO/RAE latent may be nearly fixed-radius. To test RAFM where radial variation is actually present, add one decodable image-latent control after the RAE experiment.

Choose one well-supported representation from relevant literature and available official code, preferably:

- DC-AE; or
- SD3-VAE.

First measure radial variability and decoder sensitivity exactly as in Phase 2.

Run at least the same four source/path variants on a feasible dataset/configuration. ImageNet-256 is preferred if compute permits; otherwise use a clearly stated smaller benchmark.

This control is important because:

- RAE/DINO can test whether RAFM reduces correctly to angular flow in a fixed-radius regime;
- a non-degenerate latent can test whether matching radii adds value beyond fixed-radius spherical flow.

Do not add multiple latent spaces before completing one rigorous control.
